# Equip Health

> Equip Health is a virtual, evidence-based eating disorder treatment provider (founded 2019 by Kristina Saffran and Erin Parks) delivering 100% remote, insurance-covered care for all eating disorder diagnoses across the United States, with a five-person care team model (therapist, medical provider, dietitian, family mentor, peer mentor). Equip publishes no public developer API, SDK, or documentation program.
